Account / Delete

Delete your account

TK Hub LLC publishes RADR. This page is public. You do not need the app installed, and you do not need to sign in, to ask for deletion.

In the app

  1. Open RADR and go to Account. On iPhone and Android that is Profile, then Account.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Type DELETE.
  4. Confirm. On Android the button reads DELETE FOREVER. On iPhone it reads Delete Account.

That destroys the sign-in and the synced copy immediately. It does not erase recordings, clips or photos that live only on the phone.

What is deleted

  • The sign-in itself: email and password, Google, or Apple. The login stops existing.
  • The synced vault: driver card (name, contact, licence, vehicle, insurance, and any health details you typed, including blood type, allergies, conditions, medications and organ-donor status), Signal balance, and trip and case history.
  • The public username, which is released so someone else can use it.

This happens at once when the in-app deletion succeeds. A vault that is never updated and never explicitly deleted is removed after two years.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Recordings, clips, photos and trips that exist only on the phone stay until you delete them in the app or delete the app. Account deletion does not wipe the phone.
  • Crash reports are not tied to an account. A report is deleted thirty days after the last matching event. You can turn crash reports off in Profile under Send crash reports. They are on until you do.
  • A live ETA link expires when you set it to, and never lasts longer than twelve hours. You can revoke it sooner. After that the link shows nothing.
  • Ticket photographs uploaded so law firms can bid, Play purchase records, and encrypted message ciphertext already relayed are stored apart from the vault. The in-app button does not erase those. The email below does.

If you cannot open the app

Email support.radr@tkhub.app from the address on the account and ask us to delete it. Include the email the account uses and whether it was iPhone or Android. A person reads it and deletes the sign-in, the synced vault, uploaded ticket photographs, purchase records tied to the account, and relayed message records.

Email a deletion request

Deleting a RADR account deletes only RADR's data. It does not touch any other TK Hub app.
