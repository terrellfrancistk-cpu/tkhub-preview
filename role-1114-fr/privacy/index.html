Privacy Policy

TK Hub LLC · ROLE · tryrole.app · Effective January 1, 2026

TK Hub LLC (“we”) runs ROLE. This policy says what we keep about your account and what we do not do with it.

What we keep

We keep the account you create, the resume and facts you upload, the job packets written from that resume, and the answers you save for company career pages. Crash reports stay on the account that hit them. We do not copy another person’s resume onto your account.

What we do not do

We do not sell your resume. We do not invent employers, titles, or numbers. We do not log into LinkedIn or Indeed as you. Apply passwords, when you choose to save them, are stored only so ROLE can open a company career page you authorized. They are not shown back on the tracker.

Mail

If you connect a mailbox, ROLE reads messages from employers and job sites you applied to. Other mail is ignored and not stored.

Job boards, only if you turn it on

Off by default. If you turn on “Let ROLE sign in for me” for a job board in Settings and save that board’s login, ROLE’s server signs in to that board with it and applies there for you. The password is stored sealed, the board’s session is stored sealed, and a sign-in code the board emails you is read only to finish that sign-in. Turning the switch off stops it and forgets the board session.

Visits to the website

When a page opens, ROLE records the time, the page, the site you came from, an approximate place from the network (city, region, country), the kind of device and browser, whether you were signed in, and a random ID kept in your browser. This visit log does not store your IP address, does not follow you to other sites, and uses no advertising cookies. Only the owner of ROLE can see it.

Who runs ROLE

TK Hub LLC, a Delaware limited liability company based in Texas, runs ROLE at tryrole.app. Texas law and venue stay on the terms page.

Purposes and legal basis

We keep an account so you can sign in (contract). We store the resume, facts, packets, and answers you give us so ROLE can write and send applications you ask for (contract and your instructions). We keep receipts of those sends (contract). We keep crash reports to fix the app (legitimate interest). Sign-in with Google, Apple, or X is only to open the account you chose.

How long

The account, resume, jobs, packets, and receipts stay until you delete the account. Crash rows are kept with that account and go with the delete. A mailbox connection stays until you disconnect it.

Who processes it

Hosting and the database store the account. Google and Apple (and X, when you use it) process sign-in. Email delivery sends messages you ask for. Stripe processes a card on the website. They act on our instructions.

The App Store and Google Play process purchases in the iPhone and Android apps. To write and score your resume and cover letter, ROLE sends your resume text and the posting to an AI provider: xAI or Google (Gemini), or the provider whose key you add in Settings. They act on our instructions too.

International transfers

If a processor stores data outside your country, the transfer uses Standard Contractual Clauses.

Your rights

You can access, correct, delete, and export your data, object to a use, restrict a use, and withdraw consent. Download my data is in Settings. Delete account is in Settings and explained at /delete-account. You can complain to a data-protection authority.

Questions go to hunt@tkhub.app.
