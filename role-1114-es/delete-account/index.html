Delete account

Sign in, open Settings, and use Delete account. Type DELETE. That removes the ROLE profile, resumes, jobs, packets, and receipts, and it also removes the sign-in record: the user, sessions, linked accounts, and verification rows.

Download my data is on the same Settings page. It saves a JSON file of the profile, resumes, jobs, packets, and receipts before you delete.

Questions go to hunt@tkhub.app.
