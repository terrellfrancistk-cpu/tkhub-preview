# TK Hub logo (vector master)

`tkhub-logo.svg`, `tkhub-logo-on-black.svg`, `tkhub-mark.svg`, `tkhub-mark-on-black.svg` and the
512/1024 PNG exports are Terrell's Nov 14, 2026 vector rebuild of the TK Hub logo (same look as the

folder in the tkhub-preview repository. Brand cyan `#00CBF6` on black. Use these, never the old
300 px raster or an upscale of it.
