Published standards against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) for Zilt and every TK Hub app. Last updated September 20, 2026. This page is public. No login.

TK Hub LLC products include Zilt, Booked, Curb, Afterglow, Our People, WRACE, RADR, NOIR, and 4AM Files. Operator: Terrell Francis. Texas. Contact: terrell.francis@tkhub.app · 469-903-1262 · tkhub.app.

Age

Zilt, Afterglow, NOIR, and any dating or after-hours layer are 18+ only. Minors cannot create an account, post, chat, or use Meet / After Hours. If we learn an account belongs to someone under 18 on an 18+ product, we remove it.

What we ban

We do not allow child sexual abuse or exploitation material in any form, including CSAM, grooming, sexual content involving anyone under 18, or attempts to contact a minor for sex. That ban covers posts, video, chat, profiles, comments, live, and links.

In the app: open the profile, post, or chat → Report. You do not need a paid plan to report.

Email: terrell.francis@tkhub.app with the subject “Child safety report”. Include the username or URL and what you saw. Do not attach illegal images.

Phone: 469-903-1262.

We review reports. We remove violating content and accounts. We preserve records needed for law enforcement. We report CSAM to NCMEC and to regional or national authorities as required by law. The designated contact for these practices is Terrell Francis at terrell.francis@tkhub.app.
