BOOKED — BUSINESS CARD DESIGN BRIEF
Paste everything below into your AI design tool. Ask it for a print-ready PDF,
front and back, 3.5 x 2 in at 300 DPI with 0.125 in bleed.
====================================================================

You are designing a double-sided business card for an iPhone and Android
app called Booked. Follow this brief exactly. Do not invent new colours,
new fees, or new taglines. Every number below is what the app actually
charges.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. TRIM, BLEED AND SAFE AREA
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Finished size      3.5 x 2 inches (US standard), LANDSCAPE
Resolution         300 DPI  -> 1050 x 600 pixels at trim
Bleed              0.125 in on all four sides
Full artboard      3.75 x 2.25 in -> 1126 x 676 pixels
Safe margin        Keep all text at least 0.125 in (38 px) inside trim
Sides              2 (front and back). Same trim for both.
Colour mode        Deliver CMYK for print; the hex values below are the
                   RGB masters, convert them, do not re-pick them.
Output             One PDF, 2 pages: page 1 = front, page 2 = back.
                   Fonts embedded or outlined. No crop marks needed
                   unless the printer asks.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
2. EXACT COLOUR PALETTE (these are the app's real values)
--------------------------------------------------------------------

Highlight tint    #FFD6BE    255, 214, 190     Small kicker text ON terracotta.

Colour rules:
- Only two background colours exist: paper #F6F4EF and terracotta deep #A5461A.
  Nothing else. No gradients across the whole card, no purple, no blue,
  no black slab, no drop shadows on text.
- Terracotta is an accent, not a wash. On the paper side it appears only as
  a very faint blurred glow bleeding off the right edge and in small
  elements, never as a heavy panel.
- If asked for a Pantone match, terracotta deep is closest to Pantone 1675 C.

3. TYPEFACES
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Display / wordmark   Source Serif 4, weight Black. Free from Google Fonts.
                     Acceptable substitutes if unavailable, in order:
                     Source Serif Pro Black, Newsreader ExtraBold,
                     Spectral ExtraBold. Never a slab serif, never Playfair,
                     never a script.
Tagline              Source Serif 4 Italic, regular weight.
All other text       Inter. Regular for body, Medium and SemiBold for
                     emphasis, Bold for small caps labels.
                     Substitute: Söhne, Suisse Int'l, or Helvetica Now.
                     Never Montserrat, never Poppins, never Space Grotesk.

Type sizes at 300 DPI (pixels):
Wordmark "Booked"    104 px, Source Serif Black, colour #1F1B16
Tagline              34 px, Source Serif Italic, colour #6E655A
Headline on back     52 px, Source Serif Black, colour #F6F4EF
Money headline       34 px, Source Serif Bold, colour #1F1B16
Lead body line       30 px, Inter SemiBold, colour #1F1B16
Body                 27 px, Inter Regular, colour #6E655A
Small body           23 px, Inter Regular
Small caps labels    18-21 px, Inter SemiBold/Bold, letter-spaced +1.5 to
                     +2.5 px, ALL CAPS
Fine print           19 px, Inter Regular, colour #6E655A

4. THE LOGO
--------------------------------------------------------------------
The app icon is a cream house silhouette on a terracotta field with a
serif letter B knocked out of it. Use the supplied icon file. Place it as
a rounded square (corner radius = 22.5% of its width, matching iOS) at
roughly 124 x 124 px, top-left of the FRONT.
Do not recolour it. Do not put it in a circle. Do not add a glow.

5. FRONT OF CARD — layout and exact copy
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Background: paper #F6F4EF, with a soft blurred terracotta-soft (#F8E8DC)
glow bleeding off the top-right corner and a faint paper-deep (#EFEBE1)
pool at the bottom-left. Both heavily blurred, subtle, atmospheric only.

TOP LEFT       App icon, 124 px square, rounded.
TO ITS RIGHT   Wordmark:   Booked
               Under it, italic:   Home services, done right.

DIVIDER        A 2 px hairline (#E5E0D5) spanning the full safe width,
               under the wordmark block.

MIDDLE BLOCK   Three lines, left aligned:
               Post a job. Say what you will pay.
               One verified pro takes it and sets the real price.
               No bidding wars. No five callbacks. No lead fees.
               (Line 1 in Inter SemiBold ink; lines 2 and 3 in Inter
               Regular secondary.)

BOTTOM LEFT    The Booked team                      (Source Serif Bold, ink)
               Dallas–Fort Worth                    (Inter Medium, #A5461A)
               booked@tkhub.app                    (Inter Medium, ink)
               trybooked.app  (Inter SemiBold,
                                                     #A5461A)

               The web address must be set in type, not only inside the
               QR code. Half the people handed a card will type it, and a
               QR square is the one thing on a card a human cannot read.

BOTTOM RIGHT   QR code, about 158 px square, black modules on a white
               rounded card with about 14 px of white padding around it.
               QR content (use this URL exactly):
               https://trybooked.app
               Caption underneath, right-aligned, small caps:
               SCAN FOR THE LAUNCH DATE

               Do NOT print a launch date on the card. A card outlives a
               date; the page behind the code carries a live countdown, so
               a slipped date costs nothing instead of a reprint.

6. BACK OF CARD — two versions
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Make TWO backs. Same layout, different words. The front is shared.

Shared back structure:
- Background terracotta deep #A5461A, with a slightly lighter terracotta
  (#C45C26) blurred pool in the top-left for depth.
- A paper (#F6F4EF) band across the bottom, about 196 px tall, running
  edge to edge including bleed. This band carries the money facts.
- Top-left small caps kicker in #FFD6BE.
- Top-right: the word Booked in Source Serif Black, 40 px, cream.
- Headline under the kicker: "How Booked works", 52 px, cream.
- Three numbered steps. Each step is a 40 px cream filled circle with the
  number in terracotta deep, then a bold cream title and a #FFDBC6
  detail line beside it.
- One line in #FFDBC6 sitting just above the paper band, on both backs:
  "Coming to iPhone first, Android next — scan the front for the countdown."
  The cards are being handed out before the app is downloadable, so they
  have to say so rather than let someone search a store and find nothing.
- In the paper band: a serif bold headline, a secondary sub-line, then a
  terracotta pill badge with the plan name in white small caps and the
  plan benefit beside it in ink, then a fine-print line.

7. FACTS YOU MUST NOT CHANGE
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- Customer service fee is 8%. Booked Plus members pay 0%.
- Pro fee is 10%, so pros keep 90%. Pro+ members pay 5% and keep 95%.
- Minimum job is $15.
- Joining as a pro is free. Booked never charges lead fees.
- A job goes to ONE pro. It is never resold to several pros.
- Pro+ waives Booked's own fee on cash-outs. It does NOT waive
  the card processor's fee, and it does not promise when the money arrives.
  Never write "free instant cash-out", "cash out in minutes", or "same day".
- Support email is booked@tkhub.app. That is Booked's own inbox. Do not print a personal Gmail address.
- Launch market is Dallas–Fort Worth.
- The app is NOT in the stores yet. iPhone comes first, Android follows.
  Never write "download it now" or print an App Store badge.
- The only web address is https://trybooked.app
  Do not shorten it, do not invent booked.app or booked.com.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
8. TONE
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Plain, calm, confident. Short sentences. No exclamation marks. No
marketing filler like "revolutionising" or "seamless". No emoji. No
stock photography of smiling handymen. The card is paper, ink, and one
warm terracotta block — it should feel like a well-made letterpress
card, not a flyer.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
9. PRINTING NOTES TO CARRY INTO THE FILE
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Stock     16pt or 18pt uncoated / matte. Not glossy — gloss fights this
          design.
Finish    Matte or soft-touch. No UV coating.
Sides     Double-sided, full colour.
Bleed     0.125 in, already included in the artboard.
