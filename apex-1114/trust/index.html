Trust center

BAA and SOC 2 readiness

What APEX has today, what it does not have yet, and who touches your data. Nothing here is an audit result.

Certifications we do not hold

APEX does not have a SOC 2 Type I or Type II report and is not SOC 2 certified. No auditor has examined APEX. The map below is our own readiness self-assessment.

HIPAA has no official certification, so no one can be "HIPAA certified". APEX signs a Business Associate Agreement with every practice and keeps the safeguards listed here.

APEX is not ONC-certified EHR technology.

APEX has no HITRUST, ISO 27001 or PCI DSS certification of its own. Card data is handled by Stripe, which maintains its own PCI DSS validation.

Business Associate Agreement

Version 2026-10-16.1. Every practice accepts it inside APEX (Compliance) before storing real patient data. The acceptance is recorded with the signer, time and network address, and the owner can download it.

SOC 2 readiness (self-assessed)

Mapped to the AICPA Trust Services Criteria by our own team. An outside auditor has not reviewed it.

AreaStatusToday
CC6 Logical accessin placeMulti-factor sign-in (authenticator app, passkeys), per-device trust, and server-enforced staff roles.
CC6 Encryptionin placeHTTPS/TLS in transit; sensitive chart and message fields sealed with AES-256-GCM at rest.
CC7 Monitoring and audit logpartialKey actions (shares, agreement acceptances, EPCS steps) are logged and exportable. No 24/7 security operations center.
CC8 Change managementpartialChanges go through pull requests with type checks, automated tests and a build before merge. No independent change-approval board.
CC9 Vendor managementpartialSubprocessors are listed below. Formal annual vendor reviews are not yet documented.
CC3 Risk assessmentnot startedNo formal written risk assessment has been completed yet.
CC1/CC2 Policies and trainingnot startedWritten security policies and staff security training records are not yet in place.
A1 AvailabilitypartialHosted on a managed platform; practices can export their data at any time. No published uptime SLA.
C1/P Confidentiality and privacyin placeNo identifiable patient data goes to outside AI services. Only sample or de-identified data reaches them, and only after the owner opts in (labeled not HIPAA compliant); an automated code check enforces that gate. Privacy policy is public.

Subprocessors

  • Stripe: Card payments for practices and patient statements
  • Twilio: Practice texts, calls and video visits (may carry patient contact details; practice-facing use only)
  • Cloudflare: Bot protection (Turnstile) on sign-in
  • Resend: Account and practice email (may carry patient contact details; practice-facing use only)
  • Telnyx: Practice texts and calls when the office uses Telnyx instead of Twilio (may carry patient contact details; practice-facing use only)
  • SendGrid or Postmark: Backup email delivery when Resend is not available (may carry patient contact details; practice-facing use only)
  • Website and database hosts: Run the APEX app and store its encrypted data (may carry patient contact details; practice-facing use only)
  • Stedi: Eligibility checks and claims, only when the office connects a clearinghouse (may carry patient contact details; practice-facing use only)
  • E-prescribing partner: Prescriptions, only when the office turns e-prescribing on (may carry patient contact details; practice-facing use only)
  • Google Cloud: Hosts the paid private AI for APEX, only when it is turned on (may carry patient contact details; practice-facing use only)
  • Outside AI services (named on the privacy page): no identifiable patient data. Only sample or de-identified data, and only after the owner opts in. Not a HIPAA business associate.
