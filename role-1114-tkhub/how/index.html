How ROLE works

Four steps. You do the first two once; ROLE does the rest and shows its work.

  1. 1

    1. Upload your resume

    Your part: One PDF

    ROLE works from the resume you already have. It never makes up employers, job titles, or tools.

  2. 2

    2. Pick three job titles

    Your part: Three titles

    ROLE searches for those titles and the next step up in the same line of work. It skips internships and jobs in other careers.

  3. 3

    3. ROLE writes your application

    Your part: Nothing

    For each job, ROLE tailors your resume to the posting and writes a cover letter from your real experience. It keeps rewriting until both score 90 out of 100, and it never sends either one below 70.

  4. 4

    4. ROLE applies

    Your part: Pick a mode once

    You choose how. Hands-Free sends each ready application on the company's own site. Review first waits until you tap Approve and send. Targeted watches a company career page you pick. Manual leaves the sending to you.

    ROLE sends automatically wherever an employer's site allows it. Only a real captcha, SMS/authenticator MFA, or a named technical error is left to you with everything prepared.

    On LinkedIn, Indeed, and other job boards, ROLE signs in only if you turn that on for that board in Settings, with your own login. It runs on ROLE's server every 15 minutes, so your phone can be off.

ROLE never

  • Invents an employer, a title, a date, or a tool
  • Sends a resume or letter that scores under 70
  • Solves a captcha or reads a text-message code
  • Says Applied when the employer did not take it
