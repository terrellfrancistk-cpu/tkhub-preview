# NOIR 3D assets: licenses

Every model here is **original to NOIR**, generated procedurally by `generate.py`
(no third-party meshes, textures or scans), and dedicated to the public domain under
**CC0 1.0** (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

Format: glTF 2.0 binary (.glb), metres, Y up, vertex colours (COLOR_0).
Copies: `web/public/3d/` and `android/app/src/main/assets/3d/`. RealityKit on
iOS 17 cannot load glTF, so `NoirScene.swift` builds the same shapes from
`MeshResource` primitives with matching dimensions and colours.

Rule: any future asset must be original or CC0 and added to this table with its source.

## cards/*.glb (52 readable card faces)
Generated by `assets/3d/generate_cards.py`; original NOIR artwork released CC0.
Rank and suit glyphs are rendered from DejaVu Sans Bold (Bitstream Vera / DejaVu
free font licence, which permits embedding rendered glyphs in artwork).

## reel.glb and keno/*.glb (slot reel drum, 80 numbered Keno balls)
Generated by `assets/3d/generate_reels_keno.py`; original NOIR artwork released CC0.
Reel symbols (7, BAR, star, diamond, heart, A, K, crown) and ball numbers are rendered
from DejaVu Sans Bold (same free font licence as the cards). Reel symbol i faces +Z when
the drum is rotated -i*45 deg about X. iOS draws the same textures with UIGraphicsImageRenderer.

## panel.glb (runtime-textured quad)
Generated by `assets/3d/generate_panel.py`; original NOIR asset released CC0. The apps
paint its texture at runtime (scratch tickets; per-game slot drum strips are painted onto
reel.glb the same way from each game's own symbols), so no third-party art is involved.
