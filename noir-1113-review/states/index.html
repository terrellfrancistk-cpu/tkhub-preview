The House Rules

Where we deal

NOIR is built for players everywhere, but the right to deal is granted one place at a time. Every country writes its own gaming law, and the United States goes further and writes it state by state — a dozen states changed their position during 2025 and 2026. So the floor opens exactly where NOIR holds a position it can stand behind, and stays shut everywhere else rather than guessing. Today that means the United States, state by state, plus the open sea; more countries open as each licence is granted. Here is the whole map, including the places we turn away.

Everywhere else, NOIR opens as a social casino. Every game and every table is there, coins cost half what they do on the licensed map, and the coins are not money: no wager is placed, no cash balance is held, and there is no cash out. Where we later hold a licence to deal for real money, that country moves onto the list above and is announced in the app — it never happens quietly.
