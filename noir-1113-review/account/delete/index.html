Delete your NOIR account

You can erase your NOIR account and everything in it at any time. Deleting removes this NOIR account only. It does not touch an account you may hold with another TK Hub app.

In the NOIR app

Open Profile (Android) or Settings (iPhone), choose Delete account, type DELETE and confirm. The account, its wallet, its ledger and its history are erased on every device.

On this website

Sign in, open Account, choose Delete account, type DELETE and confirm. It is the same delete as in the app.

Go to Account

By email

No longer have the app or the password? Write from the email address on the account and ask us to delete it. We confirm by reply before anything is erased, so nobody can delete an account that is not theirs.
