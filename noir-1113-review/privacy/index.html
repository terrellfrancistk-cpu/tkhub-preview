Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 October 2026

Not yet reviewed by counsel

This document is published for transparency and is not legal advice. It has not been cleared by Delaware and Texas counsel. NOIR runs on virtual coins with no cash out, so nothing here governs real money or protected health information — and none will be handled under it until an attorney has approved it.

The short version

TK Hub LLC, a Delaware limited liability company, is the data controller for every TK Hub app. NOIR keeps your wallet, VIP tier, house level and settings on your device. We do not sell or share personal information. We do not build advertising profiles.

No individual member, manager, officer, employee, trustee or affiliated trust of TK Hub LLC is a controller of your data or a party to this policy. Requests, claims and regulatory correspondence go to TK Hub LLC at support.noir@tkhub.app.

What stays on your device

Your gold coin and diamond balances, VIP points, house level, unlocked table felts, daily streak, challenge progress, transaction ledger, identity cards and any profile photo you add are written to the app's private storage on your phone. On iOS this is the app container; on Android it is app-private preferences. This data is never uploaded to us.

Deleting the app removes all of it permanently. There is no backup on our side and no way for us to restore a deleted wallet.
