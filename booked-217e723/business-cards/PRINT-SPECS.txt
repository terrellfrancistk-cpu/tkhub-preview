BOOKED — BUSINESS CARD PRINT SPECS
==================================

Size            3.5 x 2 in (standard US business card), landscape
Resolution      300 DPI
Bleed           0.125 in on all four sides (files marked "-bleed")
Safe area       Keep 0.125 in clear inside the trim — already respected
Colour          RGB files. Any print shop converts to CMYK; the terracotta
                (#A5461A) prints closest to Pantone 1675 C if asked.
Paper advice    16pt or 18pt uncoated / matte. The design is warm paper and
                ink — a glossy stock fights it.
Finish          Matte or soft-touch. No UV gloss.
