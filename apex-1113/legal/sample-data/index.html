Sample data used in APEX demos

These open research datasets are shown only as labelled samples. They are not your patients' records.

Translation for convenience. The English version controls. Citations stay in the language of the dataset paper. The English version of this page controls.

  1. Zhou, You; Xu, Yan; Tarce, Mihai-Adrian (2026). DentVoxel: a fully annotated dental CBCT dataset with 38 instance anatomical structures. figshare. https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.31239889.v2. CC BY 4.0. Changes: case 0007 downsampled to 0.6 mm; surfaces extracted, decimated and Draco-compressed.
  2. Wasserthal, J. (2023). Dataset with segmentations of 117 important anatomical structures in 1228 CT images (v2.0.1). Zenodo. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.10047292. CC BY 4.0. Also cite: Wasserthal et al., "TotalSegmentator", Radiology: Artificial Intelligence 2023. Changes: surfaces extracted and decimated.
  3. Shen, L. et al. (2023). OIMHS dataset. figshare. https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.23508453.v1. CC0. The dataset paper asks users to have ethics approval for research use. We use it only as a UI demo image, not for research or training.
  4. Jin, K. et al. (2022). FIVES: A Fundus Image Dataset for AI-based Vessel Segmentation. figshare. https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.19688169.v1. CC BY 4.0. Changes: resized 2048→1024 px, JPEG.
  5. Mureșanu, S., Hedeșiu, M., Iacob, L. (2025). Dataset for Automating Dental Condition Detection on Panoramic Radiographs. Zenodo. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.15487430. CC BY 4.0.
  6. Ahmed, S.M.F. et al. (2025). Annotated intraoral image dataset for dental caries detection. Zenodo. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.14827784. CC BY 4.0. Changes: re-encoded JPEG.
