Security and compliance

This page lists what APEX does today. We'd rather tell you plainly than overstate it.

Certifications

APEX does not have a SOC 2 report and is not SOC 2 certified. APEX is not ONC-certified EHR technology. HIPAA has no official certification; we sign a BAA and keep the safeguards below.

Safeguards in place

  • Business Associate Agreement. Every practice can accept APEX's standard BAA inside the app before storing real patient data.
  • Encryption in transit. The site and API are served only over HTTPS (TLS).
  • Sealed chart fields. Sensitive chart and message fields are sealed with AES-256-GCM before they are stored.
  • Sign-in protection. Multi-factor sign-in (authenticator app and passkeys) and per-device trust.
  • Role-based access. Each staff role sees only what it needs; the server enforces it, not just the screen.
  • Audit trail. Key actions such as shares and agreement acceptances are logged, and owners can review and export the log.
  • No outside AI on patient data. Patient data is not sent to outside AI services. An automated check blocks outside AI calls in the code.
  • Short-lived sharing. Tap-to-share links expire after 15 minutes.
  • Card payments. Cards are handled by Stripe. APEX does not store card numbers.
  • Your data, your exit. Practices can export their records at any time at no charge.

Report a security concern to support.apex@tkhub.app.
