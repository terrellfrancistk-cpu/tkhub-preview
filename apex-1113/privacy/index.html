Privacy Policy

Issued by TK Hub LLC. Short version below.

September 14, 2026 · v2.0.0

Who Holds The Data

TK Hub LLC operates APEX. Your practice is the controller of charts. Members, managers, and any trust holding Company interests are not the data controller.

What We Keep

Account: name, email, role, practice, sign-in method. Clinical records belong to the practice. Face and fingerprint stay on the device. We do not sell charts or train public AI on them. No real PHI until a BAA is signed.

Share And Delete

Tap-share tokens expire in 15 minutes. Codes go to email, not the public page. CSV takeout of this chair is $0. Patients ask their practice, not us. support.apex@tkhub.app

Artificial intelligence

Apex uses artificial intelligence to draft notes, read images, take voice commands, and run the council. Output is a suggestion a licensed clinician reviews before it is used in care. It is not a diagnosis, medical advice, or a treatment decision. Real patient charts stay in Apex and are not sent to a public AI model.

Legal basis, time, and rights

TK Hub LLC, a Delaware limited liability company based in Texas, runs APEX from Texas. Texas law and venue stay. We process account data to perform the contract with the practice, and clinical data only on the practice's instructions. Health data is special-category data. It is collected only for care. No real patient data is accepted until a Business Associate Agreement is signed.

Account records are kept while the chair is open and for up to seven years after it closes, unless a longer legal hold applies to a chart. Charts follow the practice's record hold, not a marketing clock. Sign-in tokens expire with the session.

Processors: the database host, the website host, Stripe for card checkout when it is turned on, and Google, Apple, and Microsoft for sign-in. They process only what that step needs. If the practice owner turns on the free AI council, de-identified images and questions go to a third-party AI provider. That step is not a HIPAA business associate. The council opt-in links to this policy. If data leaves the United States, the transfer uses Standard Contractual Clauses.

More processors, each only for its own step and only when it is in use: Cloudflare Turnstile checks that a sign-in is a person. An email service (Resend, SendGrid, or Postmark) sends sign-in codes and office mail. Twilio or Telnyx sends texts and carries calls when the office turns texting or phones on, and keeps a call recording only if the office turns recording on (it is off unless the office chooses it). Your browser's push service (from Google, Apple, or Mozilla) delivers notifications, which carry no patient data. Stedi carries eligibility checks and claims when the office connects a clearinghouse, and an e-prescribing partner carries prescriptions when the office turns e-prescribing on. The free AI council uses outside AI providers (for example xAI, Google, Groq, Mistral, or OpenRouter) only after the owner opts in, and only with sample or de-identified data. The paid private AI runs on Google Cloud for APEX. On the public marketing pages, the Meta pixel measures visits from ads, and a new practice account (never a patient) is counted once at sign-up. It sends nothing from chart or patient screens and is off inside the iPhone and Android apps.

You can ask to access, correct, delete, or export your login, to object, to restrict processing, and to withdraw consent where consent was the basis. Patients ask their practice. Staff and owners email support.apex@tkhub.app. You may complain to a data-protection authority. How to delete a login or close a chair is on the delete-account page.
